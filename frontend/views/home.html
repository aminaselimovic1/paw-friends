How adoption works

Four simple steps from browsing to bringing your pet home.

  1. 1

    Browse pets

    Look through the dogs and cats from our partner shelters and read each pet's profile and health history.

  2. 2

    Apply to adopt

    Found your match? Create an account and send an adoption application in a few clicks.

  3. 3

    Our team reviews it

    We will review your application and approve or reject it. You can follow its status in My applications.

  4. 4

    Take your pet home

    Once you are approved, visit the shelter, meet your new friend and take them home.
