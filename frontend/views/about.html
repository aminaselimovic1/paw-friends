About PawFriends

PawFriends connects shelter dogs and cats with loving homes, and keeps every pet's health history in one place.

Browse pets

Our mission

Many pets in shelters wait a long time for a home, often because people simply don't know they are there. And when a pet is adopted, its vaccination and vet history is easily scattered or lost.

PawFriends brings it all together: one place to find a pet, apply to adopt, and keep its health records organised.

  • Make adoptable pets easier to discover.
  • Help potential adopters find a suitable companion.
  • Keep important pet health information organised.
  • Support shelters and veterinary professionals with accessible records.
